## 4. ¿Qué productos vas a vender? (Describe your products)

> Personalized drinkware and gifts made to order:
> - Sublimated ceramic mugs (11 oz and 15 oz) with the customer's photo, name or message.
> - Color-changing "magic" mugs that reveal the customer's photo when filled with a hot drink.
> - Insulated stainless steel tumblers (20 oz and 30 oz), personalized by sublimation, full-color printing or laser engraving.
> - Laser-engraved leather and acrylic keychains with names or logos.
> - Laser-engraved bamboo pens and light-up logo pens.
> All items are personalized for each customer. Designs are created by us or provided by the customer; we never use trademarked characters, brands or celebrity images.

## 5. ¿Cómo haces tus productos? (Describe your production process)

> We start from blank mugs, tumblers, keychains and pens, and every piece is personalized by hand in our workshop in Katy, TX:
> 1. Design: we create or adapt the design for each order (names, dates, photos, logos) and send the customer a digital mockup to approve.
> 2. Sublimation (mugs, magic mugs, sublimation tumblers): we print the design with sublimation ink on our Epson ET-15000, wrap it around the piece by hand and heat-press it on our HTVRONT mug press. We then remove the transfer, inspect and clean every piece.
> 3. Laser engraving (tumblers, keychains, pens): we set up each piece by hand and engrave it on our xTool M2 20W laser.
> 4. Full-color printing (tumblers and other hard items): we print directly on the piece with our xTool O1 printer and its OR1 rotary attachment, which turns the tumbler while it prints.
> 5. Quality check and packing: every item is checked, cleaned and packed by us before shipping.

> En español: explica que compras las piezas en blanco y que el diseño, la personalización, el control de calidad y el empaque los haces tú con tus máquinas.

How it works

  1. Choose a product from our online shop.
  2. Upload your logo or artwork (PNG, JPG, SVG or PDF), add text and see it on the product in real time. Not sure your file is good enough? Read how to prepare your logo for printing.
  3. Place your order. We review your design before production. Most orders are ready in 3–5 business days.
  4. Get it shipped to your door. Or pick it up at our shop in Katy.

Not sure which method to choose?

DTF for logos and colorful designs on apparel, sublimation for photo mugs, laser engraving for gifts that last. Our guide explains the differences: DTF vs. embroidery vs. sublimation vs. laser engraving.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a minimum order?

No. You can order a single custom item. Ordering 6 or more of the same product unlocks volume pricing on most items.

Can you put my company logo on uniforms?

Yes. We print it in full color with DTF on polos, caps, shirts, hoodies and safety gear, on the chest, the back or both.

What file should I send?

A vector file (SVG or PDF) or a PNG with a transparent background is best. If you only have a photo or a screenshot of your logo, contact us and we will tell you if it can be used.

Do you only work with customers in Katy?

No. We are based in Katy, Texas, and we ship custom orders across the United States.

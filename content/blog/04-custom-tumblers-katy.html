Want a tumbler with your name, logo or photo? At our workshop in Katy, TX we personalize tumblers three different ways: sublimation, full-color printing and laser engraving. Each one looks different, works on different tumbler colors and fits a different kind of gift. This guide explains all three so you can pick the right one, with real prices and a quick checklist.

The short answer

  • A photo or a colorful design all the way around: sublimation (white tumblers).
  • Full color on a tumbler of any color, even black: full-color printing with our xTool O1 and its rotary attachment.
  • A logo or name that never wears off and looks premium: laser engraving with our xTool M2.

1. Sublimated tumblers: photos and full wraps

Sublimation ink turns into a gas under heat and bonds inside the special white coating of the tumbler. We print your design on our Epson ET-15000 sublimation printer, wrap it around the tumbler and finish it with heat. The design becomes part of the surface: it's smooth to the touch and won't peel or crack.

  • Best for: family photos, birthday and teacher gifts, bright patterns, designs that wrap 360°.
  • Tumbler color: white only (sublimation needs a light, coated surface).
  • Look: glossy, vivid, photo-quality.

2. Full-color printed tumblers: any tumbler color

Our xTool O1 prints full-color designs directly onto the tumbler, and the OR1 rotary attachment turns the tumbler while it prints so the design can go around it. Unlike sublimation, it prints right on top of the tumbler's surface, so it works on any tumbler color, including black and steel blue, and on almost any surface.

  • Best for: colorful company logos, team and event cups, gifts with names and graphics.
  • Tumbler color: any color.
  • Look: bright color with a slight raised feel.

3. Laser-engraved tumblers: permanent and premium

Our xTool M2 20W laser removes the powder coating to reveal the stainless steel underneath. There's no ink, so there's nothing to fade, scratch or wash off. The result is one color (the metal), clean and elegant.

  • Best for: corporate gifts, employee and client gifts, groomsmen, names and monograms, logos with clean lines.
  • Tumbler color: black, steel blue and other powder-coated colors look best (the steel shows through).
  • Look: silver metal, permanent, professional.

Quick checklist before you order

  1. Choose the look: photo/colorful (sublimation or full-color print) or elegant and permanent (engraving).
  2. Choose the tumbler color: white for sublimation; any color for full-color printing; black or steel blue look great engraved.
  3. Send your file: a logo in PNG, SVG or PDF, or a clear photo. Not sure if it's good enough? Read how to prepare your logo.
  4. Approve your free mockup. We don't make anything until you say yes.
  5. Pick it up in Katy in 3–5 business days, or we ship it.

Care tips

  • Hand wash is best for any insulated tumbler; it protects the vacuum seal and the design.
  • Avoid abrasive sponges on printed and sublimated designs.
  • Engraved tumblers need no special care: the design is the metal itself.

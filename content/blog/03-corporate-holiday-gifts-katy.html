Tips for a gift people keep

  • Keep the logo small and clean. A tasteful logo gets used; a huge one ends up in a drawer.
  • Add a short message on the back of a tumbler or mug: "Thank you for a great year" goes a long way.
  • Order more than you think. New hires and last-minute clients always appear in December, and larger quantities lower the price per piece.
  • Order early. Custom items are made to order, so give yourself time before your holiday party or client visits.
  • Send us a good file. See how to prepare your logo for printing.
